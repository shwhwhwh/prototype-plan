# Owner policy for this project

Rui Li (@shwhwhwh) instructed on 2026-10-27 that future owner-created prototypes and
owner-built derivatives default to all rights reserved with free personal study and
noncommercial experiments; commercial use needs separate written authorization.
Collaboration is welcome. Follow RIGHTS_POLICY.md and templates/owner-rights/.

Preserve already granted licenses, especially historical RN MIT material. Never claim
ownership of contributors' or third-party work merely by adding an owner notice. Require
the documented contribution grant for future accepted contributions. Apply this default
only where Rui Li owns or is authorized to license the relevant material. Maintain the
versioned provenance checker and public display when changing licensing documents.

For official distributions of RN, BC and future owner-controlled prototypes, policy 1.1 covers ALL owner-controlled code, including earlier self-owned code, not only newly added code. Preserve valid prior grants and third-party licenses; this changes the new distribution offer and does not retroactively cancel historical rights.
